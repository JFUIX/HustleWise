About HustleWise

Built on clarity, consent and craft.

HustleWise is a business discovery and execution platform with an integrated professional connection network. We're built around three promises: honest assessments, transparent data practices, and connections that respect your consent.

J

Who's behind this

HustleWise is built by a small team who've spent years helping founders and small businesses figure out their next step — and got tired of seeing good ideas stall for lack of clear direction. We're building the tool we wished existed.

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. The full 10-step assessment and your personalised report are free, and don't require an account. We only ask for contact details at the end so we can send you the report and — if you opt in — follow up about relevant support.

How is the readiness score calculated?

Each answer is scored on a 4-point scale, grouped into five categories (clarity, validation, build readiness, reach and resources). The categories are weighted differently for startups vs. established businesses, then combined into a 0–100 score. It's rules-based — not AI, not professional advice — and intended as a directional prompt, not a verdict.

Who sees my data?

Your submission is stored securely and only visible to the HustleWise team. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing. If you opt in to be connected with a provider, we share only what's needed for that introduction — and only after you've said yes.

What happens after I opt in?

If you tick the optional consent box, a member of our team may reach out by your preferred contact method about services or professionals that match the needs you told us about. Every follow-up email includes a one-click way to withdraw consent.

Can I have my data deleted?

Yes. Email privacy@hustlewise.com with your name and email, and we'll delete your submission and any related records within 30 days.

Are the providers real?

At launch, the categories shown are illustrative placeholders. Real provider profiles are added as the network grows. When a real provider is added, they appear in the directory with the same transparent categories and the same consent rules that apply to everyone — including our own agency.
